Privacy notice

Published 12 November 2026 · Patient and Care/Staff Android apps

This notice describes Chettinad Care, published for Chettinad Medical Sciences, and its supervised hospital care trial. Real-patient enrollment starts only after the hospital activates the clinical service and phone verification. Demonstration services and review evidence use synthetic records.

Information used to provide care access

The service processes the profile your doctor creates: name, hospital number, date of birth, sex, recorded mobile, contact/address details and emergency contact. It also processes clinician-authored notes and prescriptions, released results, medical documents you choose to upload, measurements, activities and care-task responses. Authentication uses OTP challenges and staff credentials, session/device information and security audit events.

How information is used and shared

Records support the hospital care workspace, patient access, care-plan follow-up and account security. Access is limited by the signed-in account and the hospital’s authorized staff roles. Render supplies hosting infrastructure. The hospital must configure its approved SMS provider before patient phone verification is enabled; that provider receives the mobile and verification message needed to deliver the code. We do not sell medical information or use it for advertising.

If you choose to open, print or share a document, the app you select receives it and may retain a copy outside Chettinad Care. No analytics or advertising SDK is used in these Android builds.

Storage and security

Network connections use HTTPS. Sign-in information and recoverable clinical drafts use encrypted, app-private Android storage. Recovery drafts remain bound to their originating staff account and encounter. Temporary opened medical documents are removed on sign-out or account change. Android device backup is disabled. Access to server documents requires authentication.

The hospital must activate persistent database/document storage and verify recovery before enrolling real patients. The public demonstration backend is not a clinical record store and must not receive real patient information. Hosting configuration and operational acceptance are recorded separately from app publication.

Retention and access closure

Closing mobile access revokes sign-in access; it does not automatically erase the hospital’s clinical record. Contact the operator before enrollment for the hospital’s record-retention arrangements and for requests to review, correct, export or delete information. Requests are reviewed by the hospital, including the distinction between closing an app account and retaining a clinical record. Do not include medical reports, an OTP or a password in the initial email.

Permissions and reminders

Notifications are optional and use generic lock-screen text. Your assigned doctor sets care-task reminder policy. Timing can vary with phone settings; open the app each day to refresh loaded tasks. The app uses the system picker for documents you select, and does not request contacts, location, microphone, SMS inbox or unrestricted photo-library access.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions or access requests, contact satyanarayanr17@gmail.com or your hospital registration desk. This page’s publication date will change when this notice is revised. The operator must review this notice against the activated hospital service before patient enrollment.
