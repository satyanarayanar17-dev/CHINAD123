Support and access

Your doctor creates your profile and enables mobile access. Sign in using the mobile recorded by the hospital. If a code is not delivered, contact the registration desk; do not share a password or OTP with anyone offering support.

Describe the app, the screen and the problem in your initial message. Do not attach medical records or send a verification code. The care team can arrange identity verification through an appropriate channel.

Close mobile access or request a data review

Request access closure or a privacy review, or contact your assigned doctor in person. The hospital verifies your identity before acting. Mobile-access closure revokes access and existing sessions. It does not automatically remove the hospital’s clinical record; ask the operator about retention, correction, export or deletion requests.

Care reminders

Your assigned doctor controls the prescribed tasks and reminder settings. You choose whether the phone may show notifications. Open the app daily to refresh the scheduled tasks.

Medical support

Chettinad Care organizes clinician-authored records and reminders. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. Consult your treating clinician for medical advice. Use the hospital’s usual emergency services for urgent symptoms.
